AIR MAIL 💖 № 01
September 29, 2026

Dearest Jo,

Life has been hard lately, and I’ve noticed that you’ve been overthinking and feeling anxious again. Things are heavy right now, yes, and you are definitely so strong. But being strong doesn't mean you have to face everything all at once. You don’t need to figure out your entire life today, and you don’t need to put it all together in one go.

You’ve already survived so many difficult days you once thought you couldn't. You have been carrying so much quietly on your shoulders, doing your absolute best even when your heart feels tired. Please give yourself permission to just pause, take a deep breath, and take things one gentle moment at a time.

You don't have to carry the weight of the world alone. Be kind to yourself today, because you are doing so much better than you give yourself credit for. 💖

The Sun in Your Smile

Some people easily assume you’re doing completely fine just because they see you smile and hear you laugh. They don’t always see the silent weight or the quiet battles tucked behind those bright eyes and cheerful expressions. They don’t know how much courage it takes for you to keep shining even when things feel heavy.

“

Sometimes, that sweet smile of yours is the quiet reason someone else finds the strength to smile, too.

Even on the days you feel weary, your presence brings so much comfort to the world around you. Your smile has helped others breathe a little easier and made their days a little brighter. You give so much light to everyone else, please remember that you deserve to receive that very same gentleness and warmth, too. 🌷✨

A Hug in Every Line

If I could wrap this letter in two arms and give you the warmest hug right now, I would. Whenever your thoughts get too loud or the world feels a bit overwhelming, let these words feel like a cozy blanket around you—warm, quiet, and completely without pressure.

Please remember to take care of your body and mind: don't skip your meals, remember to drink your milk before sleep and after waking up, and please get some good, restful sleep tonight. And don't forget to give sweet Oreo some extra gentle cuddles, sometimes cats know just how to comfort our hearts. 🐾

📌 Drink your milk, eat well, cuddle Oreo, and take it one gentle step at a time! 🥛🐱✨

Chapter III • Always in Your Corner

With Grateful Heart

I’m really grateful to have met someone like you. For your kind soul, your genuine laugh, and for the wonderful, thoughtful person you are. Getting to know you genuinely makes my days brighter and the world feel like a much warmer place.

Thank you for simply being you, through the good days and the challenging ones alike. No matter where life takes you or how uncertain things might seem right now, please remember you’ll always have someone who believes in you, appreciates you, and is cheering for you every step of the way.

Always rooting for you,

Warmly & with care ✨
